Email agents: what starts a job

An email agent works one message at a time. A message only reaches it if it passes the scope you set during setup.

  • Promotions, social and forum mail never starts a job. It is filtered out before the agent wakes up.
  • You choose the scope at setup: your whole inbox, a single label, or starred mail only. Narrow the scope and the count drops with it.
  • The preview you see before you switch it on reads up to five of your own recent messages and writes nothing.
  • One message in scope that the agent works on is one flow.

A typical professional mailbox sees 20 to 40 messages in scope a day. That is an estimate to check against your own mail, not a measured result.

Questions, answered.

Do I pay per email?

No. One subscription covers your connected mailbox. There are no per-email or per-flow charges, and no usage bill at the end of the month.

Does Aithia send email for me?

No. Aithia prepares drafts and keeps the next step in view. Sending stays your click. Aithia never sends email in this version.

Can I change my package later?

Yes. An upgrade opens as soon as your payment is confirmed. A downgrade applies at the end of the billing period you already paid for.

What happens when I cancel?

Your access continues until the end of the period you paid for. After that, Aithia stops working on your mailbox. You can cancel from your account at any time.

Which mailbox can I connect?

One connected mailbox per subscription at launch: Gmail, Outlook or your business email. Shared inboxes and several accounts under one subscription are not part of this version.
